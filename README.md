# C&C Deli Launchpad

Build a modern, mobile-first, single-page website for a local restaurant named "C&C Delicatessen". 

Color Palette: Off-white background for cleanliness, dark charcoal gray for highly readable text, and warm terracotta or chili red for primary buttons and accents.

Structure & Features needed:

1. Sticky Header: Must stay visible when scrolling down. Include the business name, anchor links to "Menu" and "Location", and a prominent "Call Now" button.

2. Daily Special Alert: A thin, easily editable text banner right below the header for daily hot food specials.

3. Hero Section: A large, appetizing placeholder image of a deli sandwich or Latin food platter. Over the image, place a bold headline and two large CTA buttons: "Order Online" and "View Menu".

4. Menu Section: Build an interactive accordion or tabbed layout to split the menu into two clear identities so users don't have to scroll endlessly. Tab 1 is "Classic Deli" (placeholder items: Bacon Egg & Cheese, Italian Combo, Wraps). Tab 2 is "Latin & Caribbean Hot Food" (placeholder items: Ecuadorian Platters, Beef Stew, Tripe).

5. Footer: Clean and simple. Include the address (40 Danbury Rd, New Milford, CT), store hours (Mon-Sat 5am-8pm, Sun 6am-3pm), and a placeholder block for a Google Maps embed.

Overall vibe: Fast, highly legible, and optimized for someone tapping on their phone while hungry in their car. No complex animations or slow-loading fluff.
